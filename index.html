About Us

Your trusted partner for beautiful, lasting interiors

Sree Karthikeya Plywood & Hardware is a one-stop destination on Old Mumbai Highway, Raidurgam, for homeowners, interior designers and carpenters across Hyderabad.

From the plywood that forms the backbone of your furniture to the laminates, handles, hinges and fittings that give it character — we help you choose the right material for every room and every budget. Our team takes the time to explain options in detail, so you can build with confidence.
